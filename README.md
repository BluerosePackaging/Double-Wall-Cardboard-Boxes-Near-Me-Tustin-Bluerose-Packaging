## Think Beyond the Box Walls

A strong carton is only one part of a successful shipment. The way the product sits inside the box also affects the overall result. If there is too much empty space, the contents may shift. If the product sits directly against the walls, impacts may transfer more easily to the item. Internal packaging should therefore work together with the corrugated structure.

Depending on the product, businesses may combine double-wall cartons with foam, bubble cushioning, corrugated inserts, partitions, paper cushioning, or other protective materials. This gives the packing team more control over the space inside the carton and allows different products to receive different levels of protection.
